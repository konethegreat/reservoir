# Language custodians

A **language custodian** is a speaker, teacher, linguist or community leader who takes responsibility for how their language is presented in Reservoir.

## Why this role exists

Machine translation of Bantu and Khoi/San languages is often poor, and no engineer should decide how a language is written or what a term means. Custodians have **authority** over their language's presentation (see [GOVERNANCE.md](https://github.com/konethegreat/reservoir/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md)).

## What custodians do

- Review and approve UI translations
- Maintain the reviewed glossary of plant names and key terms
- Decide orthography and dialect conventions
- Review AI-drafted translations and flag errors
- Advise on respectful treatment of ritual or sensitive vocabulary
- Help recruit speakers and knowledge holders

## What custodians get

- Public recognition (if desired)
- A real voice in governance
- Tools designed for the job
- A route to funding for language work where grants allow
