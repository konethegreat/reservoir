# Glossary

| Term | Meaning in Reservoir |
|---|---|
| **Entry** | A top-level unit of knowledge: a Plant, Practice, Recipe, Story, Word/Phrase, or Place. |
| **Claim** | An atomic, attributable assertion within an entry (e.g. "the leaves are brewed as a tea for coughs"). The smallest thing that can have a source, a consent level and a review status. |
| **Source** | Where a claim comes from: a published work, a dataset, or a contributor/community. |
| **Contributor** | A person who adds or edits content. |
| **Knowledge holder** | A person or community that holds traditional or indigenous knowledge. May also be a contributor. |
| **Custodian** | A person or body with recognised authority over a specific language or body of knowledge (e.g. a *language custodian*). |
| **Community** | A group with shared cultural identity and authority over its knowledge. Self-identified and, over time, verified. |
| **Consent record** | A durable record of what a contributor/community agreed to for a claim: visibility, attribution, AI-use, duration. |
| **Visibility tier** | `public`, `community`, `custodian`, or `sacred` — who may see a claim. |
| **`ai_use`** | Per-record permission: `none`, `retrieval_only`, or `training_allowed`. Defaults to the most restrictive. |
| **TK Label** | A [Local Contexts](https://localcontexts.org/) Traditional Knowledge / Biocultural label attached by a community to signal protocols for use. |
| **IKS** | Indigenous Knowledge Systems. |
| **Provenance** | The full chain of origin and change for a claim. |
| **Agent** | A purpose-built AI component with a defined job and defined limits (see [AI agents](../architecture/ai-agents.md)). |
| **Verifier** | The agent/human process that checks a claim against sources and safety data before publication. |
| **Harvest-sensitive species** | A species where publishing exact locations risks over-collection (e.g. threatened medicinal plants). Locations are blurred. |
| **ADR** | Architecture Decision Record. |
| **SADiLaR** | South African Centre for Digital Language Resources. |
| **SANBI** | South African National Biodiversity Institute. |
| **POPIA** | Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013. |
| **SASL** | South African Sign Language, an official language since 2023. |
