# Personas

Illustrative archetypes to guide design — not real individuals. To be replaced and enriched by real user research (see issue tracker, `area: research`).

## Lindiwe, 16 — the curious teenager

Lives in a township in Gauteng; speaks isiZulu at home and English at school. Has a mid-range Android with limited data. Wants to know what the plants at her gogo's house in KwaZulu-Natal are and why gogo uses them. **Needs:** instant camera ID, names in isiZulu with audio, offline use, content that doesn't feel like a textbook.

## Gogo Nomsa, 74 — the knowledge holder

Rural, speaks isiZulu, limited literacy in English, a smartphone gifted by a grandchild. Knows dozens of plants and their uses. Is wary of her knowledge being taken. **Needs:** to speak rather than type; to control who hears what; to see her name attached; to be asked respectfully, in her language, by the app *and* by family.

## Thabo, 29 — the diaspora/urban professional

Born in Limpopo, works in Cape Town, speaks Tshivenda less than he'd like. Wants to cook his mother's dishes and teach his children words. **Needs:** recipes with ingredients mapped to plants available in the city, pronunciation audio, a way to ask family to contribute.

## Dr. Naidoo, 45 — the ethnobotanist

University researcher. Cares deeply about provenance, voucher specimens, nomenclature and not putting people at risk. **Needs:** structured, citable data, taxonomic accuracy, ability to flag errors, safe-use review queues, export for research (with consent rules respected).

## Sipho, 38 — the language custodian

A teacher and community leader for siSwati. **Needs:** tools to review and approve terminology, to see what's missing, to ensure his language is not machine-translated into awkwardness, and recognition for the work.

## Ms. Jacobs, 52 — the community-programme coordinator

Runs a heritage programme in the Northern Cape and works with Khoi and San communities. **Needs:** strong community-level controls, TK Labels, the ability to restrict or remove, and confidence that nothing sacred will be exposed or used to train a model.
