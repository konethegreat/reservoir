# User journeys

*Status: draft, to be validated with real users.*

## 1. Lindiwe identifies a plant at her gogo's house

1. Opens Reservoir (offline, regional pack already downloaded).
2. Taps the camera; guidance overlay suggests a leaf close-up and a whole-plant shot.
3. Gets "Possible matches": top candidate with a confidence bar; a banner shows a similar toxic species and how to tell them apart.
4. Opens the entry; sees the name in isiZulu with audio, and "Traditionally used…" claims attributed to sources/communities.
5. Taps "Ask someone who knows" → shares the page with her gogo to add what *she* knows.

## 2. Gogo Nomsa shares what she knows

1. A family member sets up the app and her language; the app speaks to her.
2. She photographs a plant and says its name and uses aloud.
3. The Elicitor asks gentle follow-up questions in isiZulu ("Which part of the plant? Who taught you?"), clearly marked as the app, not a person.
4. A summary is read back to her for confirmation.
5. **Consent step, spoken and visual:** "Who may see this? Everyone / My community / Only keepers of this knowledge." "May the app's AI use this?" (default: no) "Show your name?" (yes/no).
6. It's saved on her phone, synced later. She sees it in her list and can change her choices or remove it at any time.

## 3. Thabo cooks his mother's dish

1. Searches a dish; finds a recipe contributed by a Tshivenda-speaking community member.
2. Ingredients map to plants; he sees substitutes available in Cape Town.
3. Plays pronunciation audio; teaches his children the words.
4. Adds his own family variation as a new, attributed claim.

## 4. Dr. Naidoo reviews safety

1. Opens the web review queue; sees claims flagged `unreviewed` on a `caution` species.
2. Compares against literature, edits the safety note, marks `reviewed`.
3. The claim publishes per its consent; the audit log records the decision.

## 5. Sipho reviews his language

1. Custodian dashboard lists missing terms and machine-draft translations awaiting review.
2. Approves, corrects or rejects; his corrections are logged as expert review.
3. A language is marked "reviewed" in the app when thresholds are met.

## 6. A community withdraws a record

1. A custodian opens the record, selects **Withdraw**, picks a reason (optional).
2. The record disappears from public views and search immediately; caches are purged; derived data is removed; a tombstone remains for audit.
3. The contributor is notified.
