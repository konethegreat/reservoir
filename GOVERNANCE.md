# Governance

Reservoir is a **community-stewarded** project. Today it is run by its founder; the structure below describes how decisions are made now and how authority is intended to move to the community.

## Roles

| Role | Who | Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| **Founder / Lead Maintainer** | [@konethegreat](https://github.com/konethegreat) | Vision, final say while the project is young, appointing maintainers |
| **Maintainers** | Appointed contributors | Review and merge, triage, release |
| **Language Custodians** | Speakers/community-nominated | Authority over terminology and presentation of their language |
| **Knowledge Advisors** | Elders, healers, practitioners, ethnobotanists | Advise on capture, protection and safety; hold a veto on cultural-sensitivity matters |
| **Contributors** | Anyone | Propose, discuss, build, document |

## Decision making

- **Everyday decisions** (bugs, docs, small features): lazy consensus in issues/PRs — a maintainer approves, silence is assent after 72 hours.
- **Architectural decisions:** an [ADR](docs/adr/) is opened as a PR; open for at least 7 days; accepted by maintainer consensus.
- **Cultural, consent and safety decisions:** require agreement from the relevant Language Custodian(s) and/or a Knowledge Advisor. **They can block a change that maintainers cannot override.** Technical convenience never outranks a community's wishes about its own knowledge.
- **Disputes:** discuss openly; if unresolved, the Lead Maintainer decides — except in the cultural/consent domain above.

## Becoming a maintainer

Sustained, high-quality contribution over time and alignment with the [principles](docs/vision/principles.md). Nominated by an existing maintainer; approved by consensus.

## Knowledge governance

How community knowledge is owned, consented, labelled and protected is specified in [`docs/architecture/knowledge-sovereignty.md`](docs/architecture/knowledge-sovereignty.md). This is a core part of governance, not a technical detail.

## Future structure

As the project matures we intend to establish an independent **non-profit / trust** with a board that includes community and knowledge-holder representation, and a **Community Knowledge Council** with binding authority over the knowledge commons. The code remains open source regardless.

## Changes to this document

By pull request, with the same rules as an architectural decision.
